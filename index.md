The Dynamic Minds Lab, led by Dr. Cameron Hassall, explores how minds adapt, learn, and make decisions across a range of real-world contexts. Using tools from psychology, neuroscience, and cognitive science, the lab investigates the dynamic processes that shape human thought, behavior, and experience.

{% include section.html %}

## Highlights

{% capture text %}

Published research on a range of topics, including:
*Decision-making and reinforcement learning: studying how people learn from rewards and feedback, and how prediction errors guide future choices.
*Feedback and reward processing: investigating neural responses to success, failure, agency, and performance feedback.
*Computational modeling of cognition: applying mathematical and computational approaches to explain behavior and neural processes.
*Reasoning and cognitive control: examining the neural mechanisms underlying intuitive and analytical thinking.
*Evidence accumulation and perceptual decision-making: exploring how sensory information is gathered and transformed into decisions.
