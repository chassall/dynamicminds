Projects

Here’s a list of out current lab projects.

Audiovisual Feedback How is audiovisual feedback processed?

We respond rapidly to audiovisual cues. What about audiovisual feedback? Our goal here is to test the effect of feedback modality on behavioural and neural responses.

Patch Foraging How does the frontal cortex support foraging decisions?

Frontal cortex is thought to support exploration by forcing us to “try something new”. Here we are asking whether this process depends on costs associated with diffent decision types.

Task Learnability Why is it important for people to feel like they're making progress?

Which is more fun, roulette or blackjack? In this project we aim to show that learnable tasks like blackjack are more fun and motivating. We will show this by comparing brain responses to rewards across learnable and unlearnable gambling tasks.

Patch Foraging How does the frontal cortex support foraging decisions?

Frontal cortex is thought to support exploration by breaking us out of an exploitative mode of decision-making to “try something new”. Here we are asking whether this process depends on costs associated with diffent decision types (i.e., to remain at or leave a patch).
