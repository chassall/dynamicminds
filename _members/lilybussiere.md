Lily is in her fourth year in the Bachelor of Arts Psychology Honours program. Her research project is focused on studying face perception and the recognition of emotions using EEG technology. The primary goal of her project is to explore the advantages of using dynamic stimuli for studying face perception of emotion. After completing her Bachelor degree, she intends to pursue a Master's degree followed by a career in clinical/counselling services for children.
