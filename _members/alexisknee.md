Alexis is a fifth-year Bachelor of Arts Honours Psychology student at MacEwan University. Her honours research, supervised by Dr. Cameron Hassall, examines how urgency influences reward-driven decision-making using electroencephalography (EEG) and standardized psychometric assessments, including measures of impulsivity and substance use. Her broader interests include cognitive neuroscience, clinical psychology, social medicine, law, and public policy, with a particular interest in understanding the individual through the interconnected dimensions of mind, body, and soul. Outside of research, Alexis is involved in community advocacy and volunteer work spanning mental health, eating disorder awareness, human rights, and the arts. She hopes to pursue graduate studies in clinical psychology, with a long-term interest in rehabilitation and developing more accessible, sustainable approaches to psychological care.
