Dr. Cameron Hassall is a psychologist and cognitive neuroscientist whose research explores how the brain supports learning, decision-making, reward processing, and adaptive behavior. He uses a combination of electroencephalography (EEG), computational modeling, and experimental psychology to investigate how people evaluate feedback, make decisions under uncertainty, and learn from experience. His work has contributed to understanding the neural mechanisms of reinforcement learning, evidence accumulation, reasoning, and cognitive control. He is affiliated with MacEwan University and has collaborated with researchers at institutions including the University of Oxford, Stanford University, and the University of Victoria. His publications span leading journals in neuroscience and psychology, with a particular emphasis on the links between brain activity and human decision-making.
